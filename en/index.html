Claro Verde is the independent practice of Jan-Pieter van den Heuvel, based in the Netherlands. I work where energy, automation and AI meet: from smart control of solar, batteries and electric vehicles to software that helps communities organise their energy transition.

What I work on

Energy and automation

My own home runs as a testbed: solar panels, a home battery and an electric car are steered every fifteen minutes on dynamic electricity prices. Everything is in production, and everything is monitored.

Software for small organisations

Working platforms for questions that are too small for a software agency and too specific for an off-the-shelf package. Delivered, secure and transferable.

How I work with AI

The software is written by AI, under my direction. That makes it fast and affordable. My work is in the process before and the checks after: sharpening the goal, deciding where a human must stay in charge, and enforcing that in the software itself.

  1. Limits in code.

    What the system is not allowed to do, it cannot do. Not an instruction, but a lock on the door.

  2. An independent reviewer.

    A second model with a fresh view and one task: find errors and gaps.

  3. Proof before going live.

    Trial runs, fault injection and a security audit before real data goes in.

  4. A human on the final call.

    Where the impact is large or irreversible, a person decides.

From practice

Neighbourhood portal for a Dutch municipality. A platform that lets a volunteer-run neighbourhood initiative organise the energy upgrade of almost 500 homes: a map of homes, contact history and reporting for the municipality. From design to security audit in ten weeks. Live with real resident data and mandatory two-step verification.
